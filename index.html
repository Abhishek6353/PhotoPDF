Privacy Policy

App: PhotoPDF: Image to PDF Effective date: August 15, 2026 Developer: Abhishek Kurmi Contact: abhiapps.support@gmail.com

PhotoPDF: Image to PDF respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

PhotoPDF: Image to PDF does not collect, transmit, store, or share your personal information, photos, or PDF files.

Photos you select and PDFs you create are processed locally on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers, because the app does not operate any servers for file processing or storage.

Camera Access

If you choose to use the camera, PhotoPDF: Image to PDF requests camera access only to capture photos that you choose to add to a PDF. Camera access is optional.

You can change or revoke camera permission at any time in your device's Settings app.

Sharing

When you choose Share / Save PDF, the app opens Apple's system share sheet. Any destination you select — such as Files, Mail, Messages, or another app — handles the PDF according to that service's own privacy policy. PhotoPDF: Image to PDF does not receive or store information about where you share a file.

Third Parties

PhotoPDF: Image to PDF does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs that collect personal data.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because PhotoPDF: Image to PDF does not collect or store your personal data, photos, or PDFs on our servers, there is no account data or stored personal information for us to access, retain, or delete.

Temporary PDF files may be created on your device solely to enable previewing and sharing. These files remain under your device's control and can be removed through normal device and app-management actions.

Children's Privacy

PhotoPDF: Image to PDF does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's privacy practices change. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
